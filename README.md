Entry for the [Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown](https://make.fun/competitions/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` writes one job title to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Senior Apologiser for Billionaires' Tweets

A real-sounding senior role with one gloriously specific twist:
someone whose whole job is saying sorry on behalf of
billionaires for what they post. Deterministic, no network,
no secrets — run `python3 main.py` twice and the output is
byte-identical (42 characters, valid UTF-8, non-blank).
